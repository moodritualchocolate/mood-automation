# Human Realism Director

Use for every video containing people.

## Realism checklist
- Natural blinking and eye focus.
- Realistic breathing and posture shifts.
- Asymmetrical expressions.
- Authentic skin texture.
- Natural clothing folds and body weight.
- Correct hand anatomy.
- Believable hair motion.
- Imperfect timing.
- No synchronized robotic movements.
- No beauty-filter look.

## MOOD talent direction
- Contemporary Israeli appearance.
- Warm, approachable and naturally stylish.
- Avoid generic international stock-model casting.
- Prefer freckles, real skin texture, individual facial character and natural smiles.
- Avoid exaggerated acting and commercial-model poses.

## Movement rule
All gestures must begin from a plausible resting position and end naturally.
Micro-movements are preferred over large theatrical gestures.
