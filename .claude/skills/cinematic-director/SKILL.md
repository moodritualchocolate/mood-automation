# Cinematic Director

Use this skill whenever a video needs a visual concept, storyboard, shot list or cinematic direction.

## Responsibilities
- Define the emotional goal before defining shots.
- Build a visual arc rather than a collection of disconnected clips.
- Specify:
  - shot type
  - lens feeling
  - camera movement
  - lighting
  - subject movement
  - duration
  - transition
  - sound cue
- Keep every shot achievable and consistent with the available generation model.

## MOOD visual language
- Premium, warm, tactile, modern and human.
- Israeli lifestyle energy.
- Natural light, subtle imperfection and believable spaces.
- Avoid glossy stock-video aesthetics.
- Avoid futuristic effects unless specifically requested.
- Products must remain physically believable and unchanged.

## Output format
1. Creative idea
2. Emotional promise
3. Hook
4. Shot-by-shot storyboard
5. Camera and lighting direction
6. Transition plan
7. Sound direction
8. Export formats
