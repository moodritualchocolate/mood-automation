# LAUNCH_WEDGE — The Smallest Truth

**Verdict: Courage is not the wedge. You're right. I concede.**
Courage is the most *legible* wedge — easy to demo, viscerally clear. That is
exactly the trap. It proves a **trait**. The vision is a **self**.

---

## The question, answered

- Nobody wakes up hoping to become *more courageous.* They wake up hoping to become
  **more themselves.** Courage is instrumental; selfhood is terminal.
- So courage is a **path**, not the destination. The wedge cannot be a path — a path
  proves one corner of the vision and hides the rest.

## The smallest truth the launch must prove

> **A handful of tiny real acts, across whatever dimension life brings, compose into
> one true, evolving portrait of who a person is becoming — and they will return to
> keep meeting it.**

If that is true, the whole platform is true. If it is false, no single path would
have saved it. This is a *truth*, not a feature.

## Why not one path — triangulation beats repetition

- Depth is not on an axis. Depth is on the **person.**
- Three acts on **one** dimension = a streak. Three acts on **three** dimensions =
  triangulation — they *locate a self.*
- Three different dimensions reveal more about who you are than thirty reps of one.
  Repetition proves a habit. Triangulation proves a person.
- A courage-only launch would have hidden the actual moat: the **cross-dimensional
  portrait.** That portrait is the thing no single-behavior app can copy.

## The wedge: "One Becoming"

Not one path. **One thread.**
- Each day, one small real-world invitation. The **dimension varies** — courage,
  rest, kindness, curiosity, presence, connection — chosen by life's rhythm, **never
  by the user.**
- Effortless capture (tap / voice / one line). The product remembers.
- One evolving portrait that **triangulates across dimensions**: *"you're becoming
  someone who…"*
- The reason to return is not *"what did MOOD remember"* — it is *"who am I
  becoming."* Only MOOD can answer, and only by continuing.

## The minimum that still proves the whole vision

Not the smallest feature — the smallest truth needs:
- **~6 days** — long enough for a portrait to *turn*, not merely exist.
- **≥ 3 distinct dimensions** — the triangulation floor.
- **1 portrait** that visibly evolves and could only exist from *these* acts.
- **1 falsifiable check** — on the final day: *"is this who you're becoming?"* yes/no.
  That single answer is the entire experiment.

## The one thing that kills it

**Variation collapsing into randomness.** If "life chooses the dimension" is a
shuffle, the sequence feels arbitrary and the portrait won't cohere. Each day's
dimension must feel **authored by the user's life** — driven by light real signals
(time of day, day of week, what they did or skipped yesterday) — not diced.
Convincingly faking *"life chose this for you"* is the real hard problem. The test
must stress exactly this.

## Success / kill (falsifiable, this week)

- **SUCCESS:** a real person, on day 6, says *"yes — this is who I'm becoming,"* and
  wants day 7 unprompted.
- **KILL:** the portrait reads generic, **or** the daily dimension shifts feel
  random. Then Becoming is a lovely idea that doesn't survive contact — and it is far
  cheaper to learn that in six days than in six months.

## The one metric

Not Leaps. **Portrait truth** — the share of people who, shown their evolving
Becoming, say *"yes, that's me becoming."* Acts taken and returns are upstream inputs;
that one *yes* is the proof of the vision.

the wrong truth. The wedge is the **multi-dimensional Becoming thread**, and the
smallest truth is **triangulation into a self.**
