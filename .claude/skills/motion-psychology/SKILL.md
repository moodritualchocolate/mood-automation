# Motion Psychology

Motion should guide attention and emotional response.

## Timing principles
- 0–1.5 sec: recognition or pattern interruption.
- 1.5–4 sec: establish meaning.
- 4–8 sec: deepen desire or reveal product.
- Final beat: give the viewer a clean emotional landing.

## Use
- Fast motion for interruption.
- Slow motion for desire and premium perception.
- Stillness for importance.
- Reveal for curiosity.
- Repetition for ritual and memory.
- Contrast for retention.

## Avoid
- Constant movement.
- Overuse of speed ramps.
- Random zooms.
- Hyperactive social-media editing.
- Motion without narrative value.
