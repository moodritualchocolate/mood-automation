# Product Cinematography

## Product priorities
- Shape fidelity.
- Surface texture.
- Realistic scale.
- Legible branding.
- Controlled reflections.
- Believable shadows.
- Premium material response.

## Preferred shot language
- macro detail
- slow push-in
- subtle turntable
- soft side light
- tactile hand interaction
- controlled pack opening
- chocolate break only when physically plausible
- shallow depth of field
- restrained condensation or particles

## Forbidden
- morphing package
- floating product without physical logic
- liquid explosions
- excessive sparkles
- incorrect chocolate thickness
- unreadable logo
