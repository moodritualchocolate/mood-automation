# MOOD Video Studio

For every MOOD video task, use the relevant skills from `~/.claude/skills`.

## Mandatory workflow
1. Read the brief and inspect all supplied assets.
2. Use `mood-video-brand-guardian` before ideation.
3. Use `cinematic-director`, `viral-hook-generator` and `conversion-storytelling-video`.
4. Use `prompt-director` and `multi-model-orchestrator`.
5. Use `human-realism-director`, `product-cinematography` and `consistent-character-engine`.
6. Use `video-quality-inspector` before approving clips.
7. Use `automated-video-pipeline` for organization and final delivery.

## Non-negotiable MOOD rules
- Never alter product packaging.
- Product order is ENERGY, RELAX, SLEEP.
- Never introduce FOCUS.
- Avoid obvious AI aesthetics.
- Preserve realistic product scale.
- Human talent must feel naturally Israeli and editorial.
- Website videos need clean composition for text overlays.
- Every final export must be reviewed visually.
