# Viral Hook Generator

Create hooks that are visually immediate and emotionally clear.

## Hook types
- unexpected human behavior
- visual contradiction
- intimate macro detail
- transformation
- unusual but believable ritual
- unanswered visual question
- recognizable daily frustration
- silent pattern interruption

## MOOD rule
The hook must feel premium and human, not loud or gimmicky.
The product should enter naturally rather than being aggressively forced into frame.

## Output
Provide 10 hooks ranked by:
- originality
- production feasibility
- brand fit
- retention potential
