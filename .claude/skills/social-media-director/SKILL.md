# Social Media Director

## Platform adaptation
### Website hero
- 5–10 sec seamless loop.
- No essential audio dependency.
- Product visible early.
- Stable composition for text overlay.
- Fast loading and minimal visual clutter.

### Reels / TikTok
- 6–20 sec.
- Strong first second.
- Human movement and clear emotional beat.
- Safe zones for captions and UI.

### Story
- 9:16.
- One idea per frame.
- Immediate CTA or product benefit.

### Paid ad
- Clear problem, proof and action.
- Multiple hook variants.
- Avoid slow intros.

## Deliverables
For every concept, specify:
- aspect ratio
- duration
- crop strategy
- caption zone
- thumbnail frame
- CTA ending
