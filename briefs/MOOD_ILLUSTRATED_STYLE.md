# MOOD Illustrated — Master Style Preamble

> Prepend this to EVERY image-generation prompt. It is the visual law.
> Written in English (image models obey English style locks best). Never translate it into the image.

## Style lock (paste verbatim before each scene prompt)

```
MOOD Illustrated — a single hand-illustrated world, as if one exceptional illustrator
drew it over years. Medium: delicate pencil linework + soft watercolor washes + a few
focused gouache accents, on real paper texture; lines slightly imperfect; visible
brushstrokes; warm natural light; muted calm palette; generous negative space; the feel
of a high-quality illustrated book for grown-ups. Emotion is small and true — never
melodramatic: no exaggerated crying, no theatrical sadness, no fake smiles, a quiet real
moment. People are everyday Israeli / Mediterranean but universal, real bodies not
models, subtle facial expressions, believable body language. No brands, logos, or
recognizable apps.

Frame: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920. Composition law: ONE emotional focus, at most two main
figures. The SCENE lives in the upper ~55% of the frame. The LOWER ~40% stays calm,
light and almost empty — it is reserved for message text and buttons added later in
code, so keep it low-contrast and uncluttered. Do NOT crop faces or important hands.

ABSOLUTE: render NO text, NO letters, NO Hebrew, NO captions, NO numbers, NO UI, NO
buttons anywhere in the image. Notebooks / notes / paper stay blank or with faint ruled
lines only.
```

## Negative prompt (append to every prompt)

```
text, hebrew text, arabic text, words, letters, captions, numbers, watermark, signature,
logo, brand, app icons, UI, buttons, frames, borders, photorealistic, photograph, realism,
stock photo, 3d render, cgi, octane, unreal, anime, manga, cartoon, comic, cel shading,
fantasy game art, childish, chibi, oversaturated, neon, hdr, plastic skin, perfect model
face, symmetrical face, extra fingers, deformed hands, cluttered background
```

## Continuity rules (per moment)
- Same character, clothes, age, hair, home, furniture, time of day, objects, colors across all states.
- Between states change ONLY what the moment asks: the phone lowers · the gaze rises · the body
  opens · distance shrinks · the light warms slightly · the other person feels seen.

## Two paths — never success / failure
- **chose differently** (present / connected): a small, quiet change — warmer light, eye contact,
  fewer separating objects, a gentle softening (never a celebration).
- **not yet**: never dark, never guilt, never disappointment; the scene stays open, the way is
  still there, a small light remains.

## The witness (evidence)
A quiet real-world change tied to THIS scene — phone placed face-down, child sitting closer, two
cups side by side, a hand resting on a shoulder, morning light entering. A symbol only when it
truly belongs. Never a generic lantern/leaf/path unless the story earns it.

## Character bible (Presence · phone/partner set)
- `character_male_35_v1` — man ~35, dark curly hair, light stubble, open blue denim shirt over a
  plain white tee, calm tired eyes.
- `character_partner_female_34_v1` — woman ~34, auburn hair loosely tied back, soft cream top.
- `location_living_room_evening_v1` — warm evening living room: wooden table, a lit lamp, a couple
  of plants, framed family photos on a shelf, a low sofa; warm amber lamplight.
