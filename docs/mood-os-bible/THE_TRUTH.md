# THE TRUTH

The meaning under the mechanics. If this document and nothing else survived, MOOD
could be rebuilt from it.

## The five, sorted

| | Role |
|---|---|
| **Moment** | The doorway. A catalyst — it exists only to provoke a choice. |
| **Life** | The arena. The board. Where it becomes real. |
| **Choice** | **The engine.** The daily ignition; agency in motion. If Choice stops, everything downstream stops. |
| **Memory** | **The consequence.** The record of choices. It preserves; it does not cause. |
| **Becoming** | **The product.** The portrait that emerges — the visible proof of the engine, and the reason people return. |

Becoming is what we sell. Choice is what powers it. Everything else is delivery.

## Strip every screen, image, animation, and word. This remains:

> ## You are not who you were told you are.
> ## You are who you keep choosing to become.

That truth — not the mechanics — is the company.

## Why this is the company, not a tagline

- It is not wellness, journaling, meditation, or even courage. Those are symptoms.
  The thing MOOD actually offers is **liberation from the fixed self** — the news
  that you are not finished, not diagnosed, not stuck.
- Every hard "never" we fought for was already this truth in disguise:
  a **score** is a verdict, a **streak** is a cage, a **label** is a sentence. Each
  one says *"this is who you are."* MOOD exists to refuse that sentence.
- It explains the moat without mentioning software: no competitor built on
  engagement, points, or advice can say *"you are not finished"* and mean it — their
  business depends on you staying exactly as measurable as you are today.

## The company in one line

> **MOOD exists to remind people they are not finished — and to let them prove it,
> one real choice at a time.**

Everything we build is only the proof. The truth came first.
