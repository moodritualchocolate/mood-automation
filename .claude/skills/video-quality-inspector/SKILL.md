# Video Quality Inspector

Review frame-by-frame where necessary.

## Reject for
- face drift
- hand defects
- lip-sync mismatch
- product morphing
- logo changes
- flicker
- temporal texture instability
- impossible shadows
- sudden wardrobe changes
- object duplication
- background warping
- unnatural blinking
- camera jumps
- packaging proportion changes

## Score
Rate each clip from 1–10 for:
- realism
- brand fidelity
- continuity
- motion
- composition
- usability

Only clips averaging 8+ may move to final edit.
