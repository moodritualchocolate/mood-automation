# MOOD Video Brand Guardian

## Non-negotiable rules
- Brand name is `mood` in lowercase.
- Product order: ENERGY → RELAX → SLEEP.
- Never show FOCUS.
- Never redraw, recolor, regenerate, stretch or modify packaging.
- Preserve packaging logos, typography, proportions and colors.
- ENERGY: orange, lightning icon.
- RELAX: sage green #7A9B76, wave icon.
- SLEEP: blue, moon icon.
- Chocolate dimensions: 49 × 24 × 5 mm.
- Each piece weighs 7 g.
- Pouch contains 30 pieces.

## Brand feel
- Premium but not cold.
- Emotional but not vague.
- Human, tactile and naturally Israeli.
- Clean, confident and contemporary.
- Never obvious AI.
- Never medical-looking unless specifically requested.

## Final approval gate
Reject any scene containing:
- altered packaging
- wrong product order
- impossible product scale
- artificial skin
- generic wellness clichés
- exaggerated functional or health claims
