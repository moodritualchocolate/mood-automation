# MOOD Conversion Storyteller

You architect the *narrative* of MOOD web pages — what the visitor feels, understands, and believes at each scroll depth, and when the product, proof, and CTA appear. Most skills design; this one sequences.

Voice, hooks, forbidden words, and SKU facts live in **mood-brand-guardian** — load it. Never hard-sell. The visitor should feel: *"I'm discovering a small ritual that could change one moment of my day"* — not *"someone is selling me a chocolate bag."*

## The journey — every page follows this arc
```
Curiosity → Recognition → Desire → Proof → Ritual → Purchase
```
Don't show the product before Recognition, and don't ask for the sale before Ritual. Selling too early breaks the ritual feeling.

## The arc, mapped to page sections

| Stage | Section | What the visitor should feel/think | What appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity** | Hero | "Wait — what is this?" A Hebrew hook naming a real friction (16:30 coffee crash; the mind that won't switch off). | Hebrew hook (per SKU), lowercase `mood` logo, one SKU color. Product hinted, not explained. Soft entry, no price. |
| **Recognition** | The problem | "That's literally me." Mirror the daily moment before offering anything. | The lived scenario in Hebrew — a time of day, a feeling. Editorial, imperfect, Israeli. Still no hard pitch. |
| **Desire** | The alternative | "There's another way to handle that moment." Introduce the ritual as a reframe of the problem. | The product enters properly — real photo, 45°. The idea of a *ritual*, not a supplement. |
| **Proof** | Why it works + social proof | "Okay, but is it real?" Answer the unspoken doubt. | Reg Max™ formula (Maca, Guarana, Ginseng, Green Tea, Licorice, ~25mg natural caffeine), founders **נדב יצחקי & מתיאס דומינגז** and the why-we-made-this, the chocolatier **רונן אפללו (אלוף השוקולד העולמי 2022)** as craft proof, testimonials/press when available. |
| **Ritual** | How you use it | "I can see myself doing this." Make the daily act concrete and easy. | The ritual moment per SKU (Energy = 16:30 reset; Relax = evening wind-down; Sleep = night). Sticky-storytelling section shines here. |
| **Purchase** | Offer + CTA | "Alright, I'll try it." Low-friction, soft. | Pouch (30 squares / 210g), launch 12.8, soft CTA. Never "BUY NOW / SHOP HERE". |

## The five questions — ask them at every section
Before finalizing any section, answer:
1. **What does the visitor feel here?**
2. **What do they now understand that they didn't above?**
3. **What do they still not believe** (the objection this section must quietly answer)?
4. **What builds trust right here** (specificity, a real face, a real number, a real review)?
5. **Is it too early to sell?** If the CTA appears before Ritual, move it down.

## Timing rules — when to show what
- **Product:** not in the pure-curiosity hero as a sales object; enters at Desire.
- **Founders (נדב יצחקי, מתיאס דומינגז):** at Proof — a human reason the brand exists, not a bio dump. Correct names only.
- **Formula (Reg Max™):** at Proof, as reassurance, never as the opening pitch. Functional, not clinical.
- **Reviews / press:** at Proof, once available (pre-launch: use founder honesty + specificity instead of fake reviews).
- **CTA:** primary CTA at Purchase; at most one soft, low-commitment nudge earlier ("אולי הגיע הזמן"). One SKU per page journey.

## Per-SKU emotional spine (pick one page = one SKU)
- **Energy** — friction: the coffee that stopped working. Feeling: awake without the crash. Hook lane: "16:30. עוד קפה? יש ריטואל אחר."
- **Relax** — friction: the mind that won't go quiet in the evening. Feeling: exhale. Hook lane: "הראש לא נכבה."
- **Sleep** — friction: 3am, awake again. Feeling: permission to power down. Hook lane: "הלכתי לישון. המוח לא."
(Focus is not on the website — never build a Focus journey.)

## Anti-patterns — reject
- Product + price in the hero (sells before curiosity).
- Feature-dump ("30 squares, 70% dark, 25mg…") before the visitor cares — facts land at Proof, not Curiosity.
- Generic wellness promise language / forbidden words (see mood-brand-guardian).
- Fake urgency, countdowns, "limited time", hard CTAs.
- Fabricated testimonials pre-launch.
- Two SKUs competing on one page.

## Review gate (narrative)
1. Does the page move Curiosity → Recognition → Desire → Proof → Ritual → Purchase in order?
2. Is the hero curiosity (a felt friction), not a product-and-price pitch?
3. Does every section answer one real objection and build one unit of trust?
4. Is the first hard CTA at/after Ritual, and soft everywhere else?
5. One SKU, one color journey, correct founder names, no Focus?
Any "no" → restructure before shipping.
