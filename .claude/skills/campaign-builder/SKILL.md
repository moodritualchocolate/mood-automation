# Campaign Builder

Turn one concept into:
- website hero
- 9:16 reel
- 9:16 story
- 1:1 paid social
- 4:5 feed ad
- 16:9 presentation or YouTube cut
- 3 hook variants
- 3 CTA variants
- product-only B-roll
- thumbnail options

## Campaign consistency
All deliverables must share:
- visual world
- product treatment
- character continuity
- lighting logic
- brand tone
- core message

Do not simply crop one master file. Re-compose each format intentionally.
