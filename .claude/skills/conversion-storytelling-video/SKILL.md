# Conversion Storytelling for Video

Use this sequence:
Recognition → Desire → Proof → Ritual → Action

## Questions for every scene
- What should the viewer feel?
- What should they understand?
- Which doubt is being removed?
- What makes the next scene feel necessary?

## MOOD principles
- Sell a daily ritual, not only chocolate.
- Show use in a real moment.
- Use proof without becoming clinical.
- Avoid exaggerated health promises.
- End with emotional clarity, not pressure.
